## 🚀 Step-by-Step Demo Flow

### Step 1: Responsive Slide Sidebar Navigation (UI/UX)
1.  Log in as **ajay_admin** or **hod1**.
2.  **Desktop View**: Show the sleek vertical sidebar on the left. Explain that we transitioned from the top bar to a modern vertical layout to align with premium enterprise dashboard designs.
3.  **Mobile View**: Right-click the browser, select **Inspect**, and switch to a mobile screen simulation (or open it on a mobile device).
    *   Show that the sidebar automatically collapses off-screen to keep the layout clean.
    *   Click the **Hamburger menu button** at the top-left to slide out the sidebar smoothly.
    *   Tap outside on the dim backdrop to close it.

---

### Step 2: Simulate Inventory Shortages
1.  Open your command line in the project root.
2.  Run the simulation script to create active deficits in the network:
    ```bash
    python backend/scripts/create_shortages.py
    ```
3.  Explain to your manager: *"This script simulates real-time stock issues. It creates global scarcity for 5 products (forcing production orders) and 5 local shortages (triggering inter-depot transfers)."*

---

### Step 3: AI Rebalancing & Origin Verification
1.  In the app, navigate to **Rebalancing** (or the **AI Rebalancing Engine** page).
2.  Click **Run AI Rebalancing Engine** to execute calculations.
3.  Scroll down to **Active Shortage Depots** to see the simulated deficits.
4.  Scroll to **Production Planning Recommendations**:
    *   Show the new **Transfer From / Origin** column.
    *   Point out that these records display **Factory Production**, making it clear where the stock is generated when transfers aren't possible.
    *   Point out the destination **State sub-labels** (e.g. Karnataka, Tamil Nadu) under the Depot names.

---

### Step 4: Route Cost Comparison & Optimization Boost
1.  Navigate to the **Transfer Recs** (Inter-Depot Stock Transfers) page.
2.  Look at the list of AI-recommended transfers.
3.  Click the **Compare Route** button on any route row or grid card.
4.  A benchmarking window will slide open:
    *   **Cost Savings Graph**: Show the difference between the **SmartChain AI Route** vs. the **Traditional Ad-hoc Route**.
    *   **Metrics Compare**: Compare Distance, Lead time, and match scores.
    *   **Highlight the Boost Badge**: Show the calculation banner reading:
        `🚀 +62% Cost Optimization & +33.3% Faster Delivery`

---

### Step 5: Dashboard Overview & Positive Avoided Costs
1.  Navigate to the **Dashboard** (System Control Center).
2.  Point out the **Avoided Costs** KPI card on the top right.
3.  Explain to your manager: *"We successfully resolved the negative value bug. Avoided Costs is mapped to the gross production value avoided (units saved × baseline price) rather than net savings, meaning it remains positive (e.g. ₹185.4k) and accurately represents the values generated by the AI rebalancing engine."*
